Find local businesses that need you, and reach them in their language.

Nexus Reach pulls clinics, salons, restaurants and shops from Google Maps, checks which ones fit what you sell, writes a personal first message for each, and sends it by email — or hands you WhatsApp and Instagram messages to send in one click. Built for agencies selling to local businesses in the UAE, India and beyond.

Create a free accountSee how it works

1. Find

Search Google Maps by trade and city — hundreds of places at a time — or import any spreadsheet (JustDial, IndiaMART, Outscraper, your own). Duplicates and chain branches are sorted out for you.

2. Qualify

Emails and social profiles are found on each business's own website. An AI reads your idea of a good client and marks who fits, so you only write to the ones worth it.

3. Reach

A personal message per lead, sent from your own mailboxes with warm-up, pacing, follow-up sequences and one-click unsubscribe. Mobiles get WhatsApp, landlines get a call.

4. Close

Replies are picked up from your inbox and sorted: interested first, then questions, "not now" and "no". See which message and channel actually gets answers.
