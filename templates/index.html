Enrich & judge

Do all of it in one run

Enrich → judge fit → draft messages, each step only for the leads that still need it. Nothing is sent.

1. Enrich

Finds missing emails, socials, websites and owner names. Free sources first; web searches only for what's still missing.

2. Judge fit

An LLM reads your criteria and marks each lead Fits or Rejected. Anything it can't judge stays blank and is retried next time.

Rejected leads are kept, not deleted — filter by Fit = Rejected to review them.

Fit criteria

Plain language: who's a good client, and what rules someone out. Each campaign can have its own; leads in campaigns without their own use default.

Outreach

1. Write the messages

One for every lead that doesn't have one yet (rejected, do-not-contact and unreachable leads are skipped).

Settings

Messaging Sending Connections Account

About you

Who's sending, what you offer, price, tone. Given to the AI with every draft.

How to reach a lead

Order of preference. A lead gets the first channel you have contact details for. (WhatsApp is only used for numbers outside the US.)

Message templates

One or more per channel. Templates matching a lead's channel are rotated through automatically.

Follow-up message

Sent to leads who haven't answered. Blanks like {business_name} work here too. Leave it empty and the AI writes a short nudge for each lead instead.

Email limits

Sending hours

Bulk sends and follow-ups only go out inside these hours, so nothing lands at 3 am. One-off sends from a lead are never blocked.

Replies

With your inbox connected, replies and bounced addresses are picked up automatically — read or unread — so nobody who answered gets a follow-up.

Fit judging

This permanently deletes leads the moment Judge fit rejects them. Off by default — rejected leads are normally kept so you can review why.
